Why cases appear here

These cases are no longer being handled automatically. The Recovery Agent has stopped the automated workflow and created a human-review escalation.

For human analysis, each escalated transaction can be traced back to its specific customer. Open the customer ID to review that customer's transaction history and recovery context.

Human review boundary: A HIGH-priority failed recovery can escalate after the first automated attempt. MEDIUM and LOW priority cases can continue through the bounded recovery window and escalate when the maximum automated attempts are reached.
